## ☕ About Me
- 🧑‍💻 Full-stack dev for clients across ASEAN. Laravel on the back, whatever the project needs on the front.
- 🤖 These days most of my work is AI-driven: assistants, document pipelines, and tools that make language models actually useful.
- 🗺️ Weirdly specific specialty: I make malls easier to navigate (yes, really — see below).
- 🎧 Off the keyboard I'm exploring music from every genre, watching movies, and getting lost in anything art-related.

## 🔨 What I'm Building
- 🏬 **Interactive Mall Directory** — touchscreen wayfinding in 2D & 3D, Dijkstra routing, three.js walkthroughs. Running live in several malls in Malaysia.
- 💬 **Mall AI Assistant** — a chat layer on top of that directory, grounded in live tenant data with RAG, so it answers from facts instead of guessing.
- 👥 **HRIS Platform** — attendance, leave, KPIs, payslips and org chart for ~80 people, as an Angular/Ionic PWA.
- 📄 **Recruitment Platform** — AI that reads uploaded CVs and pulls out work history and skills. In production.
